🔌 Connecting Your Phone for the First Time

Most first-time hiccups come from the cable or from a phone that is in the wrong mode. Two simple checks solve almost everything.

  • Use a data cable, not a charging-only cable. Some cheap cables only deliver power. If your phone never appears, try the cable that came with it.
  • Change the USB mode on the phone. Swipe down the notification shade after plugging in, tap the USB notification, and pick the option that shares files with a computer.
  • Keep the phone unlocked. Many Android phones hide their storage while the screen is locked. Unlock the phone and leave the screen on during the first connection.

❓ Common Questions

Do I need to install anything on my phone?

No. Your Android phone already knows how to share files over USB. droidshelf simply gives your computer a comfortable window to work in.

Do I need a special cable?

The cable that came with your phone is ideal. Any good-quality USB data cable will do. A cable that carries power only will not show your files.

Will my files be uploaded anywhere?

No. Transfers happen directly between your phone and your computer over the cable. Nothing is sent to an online service.

Can I copy files from computer to phone as well?

Yes. Copying works in both directions. Copy a file on your computer, navigate to the folder you want on the phone, and paste it there.

What happens if I unplug the phone in the middle of a copy?

The transfer stops. Plug the phone back in, check the destination folder, delete any incomplete file, and run the copy again.
